# 📊 Online Bookstore Data Analysis (Excel)

## Overview
This project analyzes sales, customers, inventory, and performance metrics for an online bookstore using Excel.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning (text, dates, missing values)
- Data Integration (XLOOKUP)
- Conditional Logic & Aggregation (IF, SUMIF, COUNTIF)
- Visualization & Dashboarding (charts, table alerts, formatting)

## Dataset
- Books
- Orders
- Customers

dataset is publicly available in kaggle website:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/komal1111/online-bookstore-analysis

## Key Insights
- 📈 Monthly sales trends reveal seasonal peaks
- 📚 Mystery and Science Fiction are top-selling genres
- 👥 Loyal customers contribute a significant share of revenue
- ⚠️ Several books are out of stock or critically low

## Dashboard (available in the excel file , also as separate pdf file)
- Monthly Sales Trend – Shows total sales revenue per month.
- Books Sold by Genre – Illustrates the number of books sold per genre.
- Top 20 Authors by Sales – Highlights the authors with the highest number of books sold.
- Low & Out-of-Stock Books – Identifies books with critically low or zero stock.
- Top Loyal Customers by Revenue – Displays customers with the highest total spending.
- Sales by Country – Shows total sales revenue by country.
- Top-Selling Books by Title – Displays the books with the highest number of units sold.


## Files
- `Book_store_analysis.xlsx` – Cleaned data, analysis, and dashboard
- `Bookstore_dashboards.pdf` – Dashboard visualizations
